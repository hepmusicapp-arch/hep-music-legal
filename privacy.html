Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19/10/2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how H.E.P Music collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the Bot within Discord.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, we may collect:

  • Discord user identifiers, usernames, avatar information, and server/channel IDs
  • Commands, messages, or content needed to provide the bot functionality
  • Usage logs, crash reports, and diagnostic information
  • Optional account or integration data if connected through a supported service

3. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Provide and improve music and Discord bot features
  • Diagnose errors and prevent abuse
  • Maintain security and bot reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers that help operate the Bot, such as hosting, analytics, or logging providers. We may also disclose information when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to operate the Bot, troubleshoot issues, and satisfy legal or regulatory obligations. Some data may be deleted sooner if no longer needed.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

If you want the Bot removed from a Discord server, you can remove it from that server. If you want information about your data or deletion requests, contact hep.musicapp@gmail.com.

8. Children’s Privacy

H.E.P Music is not intended for users under the age required by Discord or applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from users under that age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy over time. Any changes will be reflected by a new effective date.

10. Contact

Questions or concerns? Contact us at hep.musicapp@gmail.com.
